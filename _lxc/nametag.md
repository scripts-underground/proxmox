Personal relationships manager that helps you remember the people in your life. Track birthdays, contact info, relationships, and visualize your network as an interactive graph.

## Notes

- Create your first account via the web UI - self-hosted accounts are auto-verified.
- Uses PostgreSQL for data storage and Prisma for ORM.
