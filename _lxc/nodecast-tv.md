Modern web-based IPTV player featuring Live TV, EPG, Movies (VOD), and Series support, with hardware transcoding and OIDC SSO authentication.

## Notes

- Access the web UI at `http://<container-ip>:3000` after installation.
- GPU passthrough support is available for hardware-accelerated transcoding.
