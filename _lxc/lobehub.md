LobeHub is your Chief Agent Operator, organizing your agents into 7×24 operations by hiring, scheduling, and reporting on your entire AI team.

## Notes

- The initial build compiles from source and may take 15–30 minutes depending on hardware. Do not interrupt the process.
- Configure your `.env` file at `/opt/lobehub/.env` after installation if you need custom settings (e.g., LLM providers, authentication).
