Radicale is a small but powerful CalDAV (calendars, todo-lists) and CardDAV (contacts) server.

## Notes

- Default credentials are saved in `/root/radicale.creds` inside the container.
- Configuration is stored in `/etc/radicale/config`.
- Collections are stored in `/var/lib/radicale/collections`.
