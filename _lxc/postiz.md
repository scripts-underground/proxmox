Open-source, AI-powered social media scheduling tool. Schedule posts across multiple platforms, measure analytics, and collaborate with team members. Self-hosted alternative to Buffer and Hypefury.

## Notes

- Initial build takes 10-20 minutes depending on system resources.
- Requires at least 8 GB RAM for building.
- Access via `http://{IP}` (nginx reverse proxy on port 80).
- Helper script available: `postiz-rebuild`
