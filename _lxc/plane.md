Plane is an open-source project management platform to manage tasks, sprints, docs, and triage — an alternative to Jira, Linear, Monday, and ClickUp.
## Notes
- Requires 6GB RAM for the initial build. After installation, you can reduce to 4GB.
- The admin panel (God Mode) is available at `/god-mode`.
- Credentials and keys are stored in `~/plane.creds` inside the container.
