YOURLS is a self-hosted URL shortener that lets you run your own link shortening service with full control over your data, powerful statistics, and extensible plugin architecture.

## Notes

- Complete the setup wizard at `http://<ip>/admin/install.php` on first access.
- Admin credentials are generated automatically and stored in `/opt/yourls/user/config.php`.
