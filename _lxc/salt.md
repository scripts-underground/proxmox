Event-driven automation and configuration management platform. Automates deployment, orchestration, remote execution, and configuration management of complex IT systems.

## Notes

- Salt master ports: 4505 (publisher) and 4506 (request server).
- Minions connect to the master on port 4505/4506.
- Configuration files are in `/etc/salt/`.
