A self-hosted, open-source vehicle maintenance and fuel mileage tracker. Track service records, fuel economy, reminders, and expenses across multiple vehicles.

## Notes

- Access the web UI at `http://<ip>:5000`
- Default port is 5000 (HTTP)
- Supports multiple vehicles, users, and CSV import/export
