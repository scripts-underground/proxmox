SponsorBlock client for all YouTube TV clients. Skips sponsored segments, intros, and ads automatically.

## Notes

- Run the setup wizard inside the container to pair with your TV: `iSponsorBlockTV setup`
- Supports Apple TV, Samsung TV (Tizen), LG TV (WebOS), Android TV, Chromecast, Google TV, Roku, Fire TV, and more.
