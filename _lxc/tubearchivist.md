A self-hosted YouTube archiving solution. Automatically downloads your favorite YouTube channels and playlists to watch offline via a web interface.

## Notes

- Credentials are stored in `~/tubearchivist.creds` inside the container.
- Web UI available at `http://<ip>:8000` after installation completes.
