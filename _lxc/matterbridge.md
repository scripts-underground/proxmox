Matterbridge is a Matter plugin manager. It creates a Matter bridge device that can be paired with any ecosystem supporting Matter, such as Apple Home, Google Home, Amazon Alexa, SmartThings, or Home Assistant, allowing you to have all your Matter devices up and running in minutes without dealing with individual device pairing.
